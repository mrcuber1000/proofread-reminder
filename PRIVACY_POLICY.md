# Privacy Policy for Proofread Reminder

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Proofread Reminder does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or browsing information. All checklist progress is stored locally on your device using Chrome's local storage API and is never sent to any server. This extension does not use analytics, cookies, or tracking of any kind.

## Data Usage

- **Local storage**: The extension saves your checklist progress per document using `chrome.storage.local`. This data never leaves your device.
- **No remote servers**: The extension makes no network requests of any kind.
- **No personal information**: The extension does not access, read, or collect any document content, browsing history, or personal information.

## Permissions

- **Host permission (docs.google.com)**: Used solely to inject the checklist UI into Google Docs pages.
- **Storage permission**: Used solely to save checklist progress locally on your device.
